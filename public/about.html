About

QR menus for restaurants

Beyourown Menu is a QR-code menu and ordering service for restaurants and cafés. A restaurant puts a printed QR code on each table. Guests scan it with their phone camera and the restaurant’s menu opens in the browser — no app to install, no account to create.

What guests can do

  • Browse the menu and see what is available today.
  • Place an order that goes straight to the kitchen.
  • Call a waiter, and view, split or pay the table bill.

What restaurants get

  • A live order board for the kitchen and floor staff.
  • Their own menu, prices and availability, editable at any time.
  • Printable table QR codes and a home page of their own.

Who we are

Beyourown Menu is operated by Beyourown, an independent software team building tools for small restaurants. Questions? See the contact page.

This site only shows menus that restaurants publish themselves. It never asks guests for passwords, card numbers or bank details.
