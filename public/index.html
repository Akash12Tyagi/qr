    At the table

    The menu, as a book.

    Scan the code on the standee and a cloth-bound menu opens on your phone. Scroll and the pages turn. Every chapter begins with a plate print; every dish can be turned in 3D, added to your order and sent to the kitchen in one tap.

    • Swipe, scroll or tap to turn — and back again
    • Live “sold out” the moment the kitchen says so
    • Track your order from placed to served
    • Call for water, cutlery or the bill without waving
    Open the menu book

    How it works

    From table to kitchen in four moves.

    1. 01

      Scan

      Every table has its own printed QR standee. The phone camera opens the menu — already knowing which table you’re at.

    2. 02

      Browse

      Turn the pages of the menu book, or switch to the searchable list. Spice levels, veg marks and prep times on every dish.

    3. 03

      Order

      Add dishes, leave a note for the chef and send. Prices and taxes are always calculated by the restaurant, not the phone.

    4. 04

      Enjoy

      Watch your order move from the pass to your table, live. Need something? One tap calls the floor team.
