The menu lives
on the table.

A printed standee with a QR code. Guests point their camera, the menu opens in the browser, and the order lands in your kitchen — no app, no waiting for the waiter.

Try the guest menu → Open kitchen console

Tip: open the console in one tab and the menu in another — orders appear live.

From table to kitchen in four taps

  1. 01
    📷

    Scan

    Any phone camera reads the table’s QR. It encodes the table number, so you always know where to serve.

  2. 02
    📖

    Browse

    A fast, beautiful menu with search, veg filters, spice levels and a 3D view of every plate.

  3. 03
    🛒

    Order

    Add dishes, leave kitchen notes, see the GST-inclusive total and send it — prices are verified on the server.

  4. 04
    🛎️

    Track

    Live status from “accepted” to “served”. Need water or the bill? One tap pings the floor staff.

Everything a busy floor needs

⚡

Real-time kitchen board

Orders stream into a kanban — New, In the kitchen, Ready, Served — with a chime, waiter call alerts and one-tap status updates that sync to the guest’s phone instantly.

🔳

Print-ready QR standees

A6 acrylic inserts, folded table tents or card grids. High error-correction codes with your brand badge.

🚫

86 a dish in a second

Toggle “sold out” and it updates on every phone. No reprinting menus, ever.

🧊

3D plated dishes

No food photos yet? Every dish renders as an interactive 3D plate.

📈

Daily insights

Revenue by hour, top dishes and average ticket — at a glance.

Made for phones
