Brand guidelines · v1

Ink, saffron and real food.

Ember & Saffron is a modern Indian kitchen cooked over fire. The brand is the room at night — ink-dark, warm, unhurried — lit by one colour: saffron, the spice we’re named after. Everything we make, from a printed table standee to the menu on your phone, uses the same two colours, two typefaces and honest photography.

01

Logo

A saffron sun with an ink flame — the ember and the spice in one mark. Pair it with the Poppins wordmark; the ampersand is always saffron.

04

Imagery

Real food, really served. Warm light, close crops, the plate as the hero. No stock clichés, no filters, no emoji, no illustrations standing in for dishes.

Crop 4:5Portrait for prints and the menu book; 1:1 for thumbnails.
Natural lightWarm, directional, honest colour. The food should look like it does at the table.
NeverOverlay text on the food, tint photos saffron, or use emoji as food.
