Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Guests

  • Orders: the dishes, table, an optional first name you type, and the time. They are kept by the restaurant so it can cook and bill your table.
  • Location (only if the restaurant turns on the check): when you place an order your browser asks permission to share your location, once, to confirm you are at the restaurant. We use it for that single check and do not store it; only a rounded distance is kept with the order. If you refuse, call a waiter.
  • Browser storage: small settings such as your theme and your open bill are kept on your own device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We never ask guests for passwords, card numbers or bank details. Payments are made to the restaurant’s own UPI ID or at the table.

Restaurant staff

  • Name, email and a salted, hashed password for sign-in, and a secure session cookie while you are signed in.
  • The menu, prices and orders you manage.

Sharing

We do not sell data. Order data is visible to the restaurant you ordered from. Our hosting and database providers process data only to run the service.

Your choices

Ask the restaurant or write to hello@beyourown.in to see or delete your data. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above.
